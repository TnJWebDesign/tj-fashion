# T&J Fashion — Design Brainstorm

## Context
Car-themed clothing brand. Collections: JDM, F1, American Muscle, China Vehicles, EVs/Tesla, Supercars, Hypercars, Muscle Cars, Shitboxes.

<idea>
**Design Movement:** Industrial Brutalism meets Motorsport Livery

**Core Principles:**
- Raw, mechanical energy — exposed grid lines, heavy borders, bold type
- Livery-inspired color blocking (racing stripes, diagonal cuts)
- High contrast: near-black backgrounds with electric accent colors
- Asymmetric layouts that feel like a car dashboard

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: #0A0A0A (carbon black)
- Primary accent: #E8FF00 (racing yellow / safety car yellow)
- Secondary: #FF2D00 (brake caliper red)
- Text: #F0F0F0 (titanium white)
- Emotion: Power, speed, aggression — the colors of motorsport

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Diagonal section dividers (clip-path polygon cuts)
- Full-bleed hero with overlapping text blocks
- Horizontal scrolling collection strips
- Asymmetric product grid (1 large + 3 small)

**Signature Elements:**
- Racing stripe dividers (thin diagonal yellow lines)
- Carbon fiber texture overlays on cards
- Speedometer / tachometer inspired progress indicators

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover: cards tilt slightly (3D perspective transform)
- Buttons: press scale(0.97) + color flash
- Section reveals: slide-in from left (like a car entering frame)

**Animation:**
- Hero text: staggered letter-by-letter reveal at 40ms intervals
- Cards: 150ms ease-out scale + shadow on hover
- Nav: underline slides from left on hover

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Bebas Neue" — tall, condensed, motorsport feel
- Body: "DM Sans" — clean, modern, readable
- Accent labels: "Barlow Condensed" — technical, tight
</idea>

## Selected Design: **Industrial Brutalism meets Motorsport Livery**

Dark carbon-black background, electric yellow + brake-red accents, Bebas Neue display type, diagonal section cuts, racing stripe motifs. This approach best captures the raw energy of car culture across all sub-genres — from JDM to Supercars to Shitboxes.
